INCLUDED AUDIO

starter_chime.mp3 is a louder edit of the public HQ MP3 preview of:
"marshall tufton bluetooth.wav" by gecop, published 17 November 2020.
Source: https://freesound.org/people/gecop/sounds/545028/
Dedication: CC0 1.0 Universal
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The creator describes a real Marshall Tufton speaker pairing/disconnection
recording. It contains chimes, not spoken announcements. This project's edit
raises the recording by 10 dB and re-encodes it as a 192 kbps MP3. Decoded
validation found no clipped samples and a -2.4 dBFS true peak. No synthetic
speech was added. Courtesy credit is retained here.
